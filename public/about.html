About Qasr-e-Shabbir Foundation

Serving Humanity with Islamic Compassion, Integrity and Transparency

OUR MISSION

Our Core Mission

Our mission is to support orphaned children in continuing their education and to assist deserving families through financial aid, food assistance, and compassionate community welfare initiatives across Pakistan.

"Whoever caresses the head of an orphan purely for the sake of Allah, will have a reward for every hair touched." — Prophetic Tradition

Our Vision

A society where every orphaned child has access to quality education, no deserving family sleeps hungry, and community welfare is delivered with dignity and Islamic brotherhood.

Our Core Values

Integrity & Transparency

Every penny of public donation is accounted for and directed towards verified beneficiaries.

Preserving Beneficiary Dignity

We never compromise the honor or privacy of vulnerable children and families.

Education First

Educating an orphan transforms not just one life, but generations to come.

Islamic Charity Principles

Upholding the noble traditions of Zakat, Sadaqah, and compassionate care.

QASR-E-SHABBIR FOUNDATION

Together for a Brighter Tomorrow

Dedicated to the educational sponsorship of orphaned children and providing monthly food and financial welfare to vulnerable families in Pakistan.
